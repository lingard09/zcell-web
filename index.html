About Us

Redefining Urban Spaces

Zcell is a smart bench platform designed to integrate seamlessly into city parks and streets. We combine renewable energy, real-time environmental monitoring, and microalgae carbon reduction technology to create greener, smarter cities.

Air Quality Monitoring

Real-time PM2.5, PM10, and NO2 tracking. Delivering highly accurate atmospheric data straight from urban smart benches.

Microalgae Reactor

Active carbon reduction through integrated microalgae cultivation. Monitored continuously via precision turbidity sensors.

Urban Heat Island

Surface temperature analysis mapping out urban heat distribution. Essential data for sustainable city planning.

Off-Grid Power

100% solar-powered operations. Clean, renewable energy driving robust IoT communication via LTE-M networks.
